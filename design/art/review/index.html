8 · Verdict checklist

  • PASS Avatars read as one family; varied ages, hair and shirts without breaking the frame.
  • PASS Activity portraits match the scene style (giraffe/lion).
  • WATCH At 32 px, braided avatars lose the braids and read as a dark shape — acceptable, but check it still reads as a person.
  • WATCH Avatar 01/05 braids extend beyond the teal circle; circular crop is doing the work.
  • WATCH Lion reads solemn next to the others; optional friendlier re-run.
  • WATCH Cover title contrast holds with the scrim, but the elephant's head is directly behind the subtitle — check at 680 px and 340 px.
  • WATCH Hero: giraffe head is near the back button; a 34 px button sits over its ear at this crop.
